FAQ

Questions from retailers.

Who can we send promos to?

Only customers who agreed to receive marketing messages from you. Unsolicited marketing to bought or scraped lists is a fast way to get your messages ignored or blocked.

How much does a promo cost?

Each message costs 1 credit per part — up to 160 characters per part — so a short promo to one customer costs ₱0.65.

We have several branches. Do we need an account for each?

No. One account sends for every branch. Use contact groups to keep each branch’s customers separate.

Can we send without connecting our POS?

Yes. Export your opt-in list as a CSV — number first column, message second — and upload it from the dashboard.

Can promos include links?

Yes, you can include a link in the message text. Keep the message short so it stays within one part when you can.
