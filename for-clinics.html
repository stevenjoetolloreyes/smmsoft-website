FAQ

Questions from clinics.

Can our booking system send reminders automatically?

Yes. Call the API when an appointment is booked and again the day before. For a whole day’s list, the CSV upload sends a different message to each patient in one go.

Should we put test results in the SMS?

Better not. Say that results are ready and how to claim them, and keep the medical details at the clinic or in your portal.

We don’t have a developer. Can we still send reminders?

Yes. Export the day’s appointments from your system or a spreadsheet as a CSV — number in the first column, message in the second — and upload it from the dashboard.

Can patients reply to the message?

Sender-name messages are one-way, so include your clinic’s phone number in the text for confirmations and cancellations.

Do credits expire or is there a monthly fee?

There are no monthly fees. You buy credits when you need them, and failed messages are refunded per network report.
