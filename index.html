FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does one SMS cost?

Credits cost ₱0.65 each. A regular message of up to 160 characters costs 1 credit per recipient. Priority and OTP messages cost 2 credits per part. Longer messages split into parts of 153 characters and are charged per part.

What name appears on my customers' phones?

Every message arrives under the sender name SMMSOFT — never a random mobile number — on Globe, Smart, DITO, Sun, TNT and TM.

How do I buy credits?

From your platform Credits page: pay by GCash, then submit the amount and your payment reference number. Credits are added to your balance as soon as the payment is verified.

Am I charged for failed messages?

No. When the network reports a message as failed, its credits are refunded to your balance.

Can I send OTPs?

Yes. Use the OTP message type in the dashboard, or kind=otp in the API. Write {otp} in your message and a code is generated for you — or pass your own code.

What phone number format should I use?

Philippine mobile numbers in 09xxxxxxxxx format. In the API you can send to one number or up to 1,000 numbers in a single call, separated by commas.

Do I need to be a developer to use SMMSOFT?

No. The web dashboard handles sending, contacts, and history with no code at all. The API is there when your own system needs to send automatically.
