FAQ

Questions from LGUs.

Can we send an alert to every resident at once?

Yes. Paste the numbers in the dashboard sender, upload them as a CSV, or pass up to 1,000 numbers in a single API call. Each recipient is charged as a normal SMS.

Do we need a developer?

No. Staff can send from the dashboard or upload a CSV from Excel or Google Sheets. The API is there if your IT team wants alerts to go out from your own systems.

Whose numbers can we send to?

Residents who gave their numbers to your barangay or LGU — through registration, a census, or a sign-up drive. Keep your list yours; do not buy number lists.

Can we write messages in Filipino or the local language?

Yes. Use standard letters and keep messages plain-text so every phone displays them correctly.

How do we pay?

Buy credits on the platform with GCash, submit your reference number, and the credits land on your balance once the payment is verified.
