Pricing

Everything runs on credits. Buy credits, spend them when you send. Credits for messages the network reports as failed are refunded to your balance.

₱0.65 per credit

No monthly fees · No setup fees · Minimum top-up: 10 credits

Message typeCostBest for
Regular1 credit per part, per recipientAnnouncements, promos, reminders
Priority2 credits per part, per recipientUrgent alerts that skip the sending queue
OTP2 credits per part, per recipientLogin codes and verification — dedicated route

One part is up to 160 characters. Longer messages are split into parts of 153 characters and charged per part.

How to buy credits

STEP 1

Pay

Send your payment by GCash to the number shown on your platform Credits page.

STEP 2

Submit the reference

Enter the credits you bought and your payment reference number.

STEP 3

Credits land

Once the payment is verified, the credits appear on your balance and you can send right away.
